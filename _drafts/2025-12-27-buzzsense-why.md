<h1 id="why">Why AI Visibility Matters?</h1>

<i>As people consult ChatGPT for purchase decisions, brands and products that don't show in those replies are practically invisible to prospects.</i>


<!--<b>If you do not show in ChatGPT (and other AI assistants) results, you are practically invisible to prospects.</b>-->
<!--<br>-->

<p>
  People are looking for brand or products related to your niche on ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude. These
  AI assistants are the becoming the trusted sources that people use to take purchase decisions like buying a device (e.g., beats headphones) or
  hiring an AI consultancy company (e.g., Runzbuzz).

</p>
<p>
  Brands have no idea whether they are recommended on not on ChatGPT and even if so, how often are they getting recommended.
  Do you think brands who do not optimize to show in ChatGPT results are going to get more or less sales?
  In other words, are invisible brands making money?!
</p>

<p>
  As it is clear that measuring AI visibility matter, let us define it more clearly.

</p>


<h1 id="what">What is AI Visibility?</h1>
<i>
  It is the frequency that your brand (or products) show in ChatGPT (and other AI assistants) answers, often measured overtime.
</i>

<p>
  Brands are interested to know how often they are recommended by ChatGPT. ChatGPT, Gemini, and other AI assistants
  pulls the information from the web and use that along with the knowledge they have within their language models to provide
  these recommendations. This means that the results are likely to change overtime (e.g., after a marketing campaign).
</p>





<h1 id="diy">Why can't I ask ChatGPT myself?</h1>
<i>
  Measurement, knowledge, and maintenance. People have to know how to run it properly in ChatGPT,
  define measurement metrics, and continuously measure the visibility of their brand (or products).
</i>
<p>
    If someone wants to run it themselves, they might start by asking ChatGPT, what are the best "niche". If they know
more prompt engineering, they might use techniques like Chain of Thought (CoT). Still, all they are going to get is
whether their brand is recommended by ChatGPT or not. They have to come up with a metric to measure the visibility.
They have to ask themselves the following questions:
</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Am I going to do it for all models for all providers?</li>
      <li>Am I going to repeat this every day? every week?</li>
      <li>How am I going to measure it?</li>
      <li>Am I going to store this in an Excel?</li>
      <li>What formulas am I going to use? </li>
      <li>How am I going to address names mismatch?</li>
      <li>How about hallucinations?</li>
      <li>How about timeouts, errors, and aggregations?</li>
    </ul>
<p>
  and much more.
</p>
